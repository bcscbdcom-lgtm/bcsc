Government links

The government websites and online portals needed for income tax, VAT, companies, import-export, licences and trademarks, in one place. All links open in a new tab.

These are the official websites of the government or regulatory bodies concerned. BCSC is not part of, or affiliated with, these bodies; the links are given only for convenience. Responsibility for the information, services and security of those sites rests with the bodies concerned. Before logging in or paying, check that the address bar shows the correct government address (for example .gov.bd). Government website addresses change from time to time; please tell us if a link does not work. Last checked: 4 November 2026.
