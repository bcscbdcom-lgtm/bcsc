Audit Support & Internal Control

Verifying accounts, improving internal controls and preparing your organisation for external audit.

What we do

For each task: the documents usually needed, estimated time, and who usually uses it.

General guidance: the information and timelines below vary with circumstances, the type of work, government procedures, law and time. They are not a contract or a guarantee; the final scope, timeline and fee are agreed in writing after discussion.

Internal audit

Transaction testing, risk assessment, control testing, findings and recommendations.

Documents usually needed
Ledgers, vouchers, bank statements, purchase and sales records, policies, approval records
Estimated time (varies)
2 days of fieldwork for a limited monthly scope; 2–5 more days for the report
Usually used by
SMEs, trading and manufacturing businesses, NGOs

Pre-audit preparation

Financial statement review, supporting schedules and audit file preparation.

Documents usually needed
Trial balance, general ledger, bank reconciliations, fixed asset register, previous audit reports
Estimated time (varies)
5–10 working days; longer depending on the state of the accounts
Usually used by
Limited companies, hospitals, trading businesses

Accounts verification

Reconciling cash, bank, receivables, payables and expenses.

Documents usually needed
Cashbook, bank statements, party ledgers, bills, receipts, vouchers
Estimated time (varies)
3–10 working days
Usually used by
Retailers, wholesalers, distributors

Stock audit support

Stock counts, stock reconciliation and inventory valuation review.

Documents usually needed
Stock register, GRNs, delivery notes, purchase invoices, count sheets, costing policy
Estimated time (varies)
3–7 days; longer for large warehouses
Usually used by
Manufacturing, warehouse and trading businesses

Internal control review

Authorisation, segregation of duties, approval matrix and fraud risk review.

Documents usually needed
SOPs, organisation chart, user access list, approval records
Estimated time (varies)
5–10 working days
Usually used by
Growing SMEs, multi-branch organisations

External audit coordination

Answering auditor queries, schedules, evidence collection and management responses.

Documents usually needed
Auditor's requirement list, financial statements, supporting records, previous findings
Estimated time (varies)
3–15 days, depending on audit scope
Usually used by
Companies, NGOs, organisations seeking investment

Monthly management review

KPIs, MIS, expense analysis, bank reconciliation and compliance monitoring.

Documents usually needed
Monthly accounts, receivables, payables, sales, purchases and bank information
Estimated time (varies)
2–5 working days each month
Usually used by
SMEs, healthcare, trading, developers

About timelines: the times above are our estimate for completing our part after receiving complete documents, not a guaranteed time. Government registration, verification, objection or approval time depends on the office concerned, is separate, and is outside our control.

Good to know

This service is audit support: reviewing your accounts, records and controls and making recommendations. Statutory audits and audit opinions required by law are given by qualified auditors or audit firms under the Companies Act, 1994; we help with preparation and coordination for that audit. Internal audit work follows the IIA Global Internal Audit Standards.

  • We do not issue statutory audit reports or audit opinions; that is the work of a qualified auditor or audit firm.

What you receive: audit report (findings, risks and recommendations), reconciliation schedules and audit file.
