RJSC Company Registration & Compliance

Company formation, post-registration changes and annual compliance under company law.

What we do

For each task: the documents usually needed, estimated time, and who usually uses it.

General guidance: the information and timelines below vary with circumstances, the type of work, government procedures, law and time. They are not a contract or a guarantee; the final scope, timeline and fee are agreed in writing after discussion.

Company name clearance

Searching, choosing and applying for the proposed name.

Documents usually needed
Proposed name, founders' identity and contact details
Estimated time (varies)
Our preparation 1 day; approval depends on RJSC
Usually used by
New entrepreneurs, startups, investors

Private limited company registration

Preparing MoA/AoA, completing Forms I, VI, IX, X and XII, applying and collecting the certificate.

Documents usually needed
NID or passport of directors and shareholders, addresses, shareholding, capital details, registered office address
Estimated time (varies)
Document preparation 3–7 days; about 1–3 weeks in total including registration
Usually used by
SMEs, trading, manufacturing and IT businesses

Annual return filing

AGM documents, Schedule X, filing of accounts and compliance review.

Documents usually needed
Registration papers, AGM minutes, shareholder and director registers, audited financial statements, Form 23B where applicable
Estimated time (varies)
3–7 working days with complete information
Usually used by
Private and public limited companies

Director changes

Appointment or resignation of directors, board resolution and Form XII filing.

Documents usually needed
Board or company resolution, directors' details, NID or passport, consent letters
Estimated time (varies)
3–10 working days; approval is separate
Usually used by
Existing limited companies

Share transfer and allotment

Share transfer documents, Form 117, share register and applicable filings.

Documents usually needed
Transfer deed, share certificate, share register, resolution, ID, applicable stamp and tax payment proof
Estimated time (varies)
5–15 working days; longer if complex
Usually used by
Shareholders, investors, family businesses

Company record amendments

Change of registered office, change of capital, MoA/AoA amendments.

Documents usually needed
Board or shareholder resolution, existing MoA/AoA, applicable forms
Estimated time (varies)
5–15 working days or more
Usually used by
Expanding or restructured companies

Certified copies and record checks

Obtaining certified copies of registration and filed documents.

Documents usually needed
Company registration details and description of the documents needed
Estimated time (varies)
About 2–7 working days
Usually used by
Banks, investors, company secretaries, tender applicants

About timelines: the times above are our estimate for completing our part after receiving complete documents, not a guaranteed time. Government registration, verification, objection or approval time depends on the office concerned, is separate, and is outside our control.

Good to know

Legal basis: Companies Act, 1994, its amendments and the applicable rules of the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC). RJSC's online system provides name clearance, registration, filing and certified copies.

  • These are RJSC's published general deadlines; we check whether they apply to your type of company and the latest amendments.
  • The 1–3 weeks for a new company is a planning estimate. RJSC approval, document corrections and, for foreign investment, banking and encashment certificates can take longer.

What you receive: registration certificate, certified MoA/AoA and forms, proof of filing and up-to-date company registers.
