For each task: the documents usually needed, estimated time, and who usually uses it.

BIN registration

VAT registration or enlistment, Mushak-2.1 application and information checks.

Documents usually needed
e-TIN, business name and address, ownership, business activity and turnover information; trade licence for our checks
Estimated time (varies)
Our preparation 1 working day; usually 3 working days including NBR verification
Usually used by
New businesses, importers, exporters, suppliers, manufacturers

Monthly VAT return

Mushak-9.1 preparation, input-output VAT adjustment and online filing.

Documents usually needed
BIN, purchase and sales registers, Mushak-6.3, input VAT evidence, challans, adjustment records
Estimated time (varies)
1–3 working days if monthly records are in order
Usually used by
VAT-registered trading, manufacturing and service businesses

Purchase and sales VAT records

Preparing and checking Mushak-6.1, 6.2 and 6.3.

Documents usually needed
Purchase and sales invoices, stock register, item-wise tax information, BIN
Estimated time (varies)
1–5 working days a month, depending on volume
Usually used by
Wholesalers, distributors, factories, retailers

VAT rate and product classification

Correct VAT treatment, exemptions, applicable SROs and rate review.

Documents usually needed
Description of goods or services, HS code where applicable, contracts, price list
Estimated time (varies)
2–5 working days; longer for complex classification
Usually used by
Manufacturing, import-export, healthcare and services

Price declaration support

Mushak-4.3 and costing documentation where applicable.

Documents usually needed
Cost sheet, raw material and production costs, overheads, pricing details
Estimated time (varies)
3–7 working days; government review is separate
Usually used by
Manufacturers

VAT audit and notice support

Notice review, reconciliation, reply preparation and hearing documents.

Documents usually needed
Notice, BIN, returns, Mushak registers, invoices, bank and stock records, treasury challans
Estimated time (varies)
Initial work 3–7 working days; resolution depends on the authority
Usually used by
Businesses selected for VAT audit, large traders, factories

About timelines: the times above are our estimate for completing our part after receiving complete documents, not a guaranteed time. Government registration, verification, objection or approval time depends on the office concerned, is separate, and is outside our control.

Good to know

Legal basis: Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012, the 2016 Rules and later amendments. VAT rates, exemptions and turnover tax depend on the business and are set by checking the latest SROs.

  • Under NBR guidance, supporting documents are not mandatory with a normal BIN application; the form must be filled in accurately. We check the trade licence and ownership papers to verify the information.
  • The monthly VAT return is usually due by the 15th of the following month. If a special order extends the time, we let you know.

What you receive: BIN certificate (Mushak-2.3), copies of filed returns, up-to-date purchase and sales registers and audit-ready records.
